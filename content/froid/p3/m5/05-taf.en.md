## Practice

**Task**:

- Identify the star connection and the delta connection on the power circuit diagram.
- Introduction and demonstration by the trainer; allocation of workstations.
- Build the circuit in an enclosure.

**Note**: the connection links are removed, because the connections are made by the contactors.

## Assessment

- Correct position of the devices and secure mounting: 15%
- Operation of the circuit: 60%
- Compliance with standards, choice of equipment and neat work: 10%
- Practical report: 15%
