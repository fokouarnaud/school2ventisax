## Practical activities

- **Task 1**: identify the different types of motors available in the lab and the store.
- **Task 2**: identify the windings of the different motors.
- **Task 3**: measure the phase and line voltages of the supply.
- **Task 4**: practise connecting motors in star and delta.

## Safety instructions

- Any work on the terminal board is done with the motor locked out: isolator open and padlocked, absence of voltage checked.
- The voltage measurements of Task 3 are made under the trainer's supervision, with a meter of a suitable category and PPE.

## Assessment criteria

- Correct identification of the motors and windings
- Correct measurements and reading of phase and line voltages
- Connection that matches the rating plate and the supply
- Compliance with the safety instructions
