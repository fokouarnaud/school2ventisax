## Reading the rating plate of an electric motor

The rating plate (plaque signalétique) of an electric motor is its identity card, issued and certified by the manufacturer. It gives the rated electrical characteristics of the motor.

| Information | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Motor type | Manufacturer's model reference |
| Speed (rpm) | Rated speed of the motor |
| Frequency (Hz) | Frequency of the supply |
| Voltages and connection | Operating voltages and the matching connection, star or delta |
| Power (kW) | Output power delivered on the motor shaft |
| Power factor (cos φ) | Used to calculate the power drawn by the motor |
| Currents (A) | Current drawn by the motor for each connection |

## Reading example

A plate shows "Δ 230 V / Y 400 V" and "6.5 A / 3.8 A":

- on a supply with a 230 V line voltage, the motor is connected in delta and draws 6.5 A;
- on a supply with a 400 V line voltage, it is connected in star and draws 3.8 A.

The motor takes the same power in both cases: only the connection changes to suit the supply.

**Note**: always read the rating plate before connecting the windings.
