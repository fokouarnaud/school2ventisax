## Equipment and tools needed (per workstation)

- 1 contactor 230/400 V
- 1 four-pole fuse switch-disconnector (L1, L2, L3, N)
- Motor circuit breaker or thermal-magnetic circuit breaker
- 1 thermal overload relay 16 A
- 1 start push button
- 1 stop push button
- 6 m of flexible cable in various colours, 2.5 mm²
- Three-phase induction motor
- Rails
- Cable trays
- 20 wood screws, 4 mm diameter

## Practice (6 h)

1. Use measuring instruments (multimeter or clamp meter) to:
   - identify the windings of the induction motor (U1-U2, V1-V2, W1-W2, or U-V-W and X-Y-Z on older motors);
   - check the insulation to frame;
   - measure the supply voltage.
2. Make the connection that suits the motor and the supply.
3. Fix the electrical components on the wiring board following the layout plan.
4. Demonstration wiring by the trainer.
5. Wiring by the learners.
6. Tests.
7. Assessment.

## Assessment

- Correct position and secure mounting: 15%
- Operation of the circuit: 60%
- Compliance with standards, choice of equipment and wiring: 10%
- Practical report: 15%
