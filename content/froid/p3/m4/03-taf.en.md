## Practical activities

**Task**: wire, in an enclosure, the semi-automatic two-direction starter of a three-phase induction motor, with electrical and mechanical interlocks, then add the two limit switches.

## Equipment (per workstation)

- 1 four-pole fuse switch-disconnector with early-break contact
- 2 three-pole contactors 230/400 V with auxiliary contacts 13-14 and 21-22
- 1 mechanical interlock between the two contactors
- 1 thermal overload relay set to the rated current of the motor
- 3 push buttons (stop S0, start direction 1 S1, start direction 2 S2)
- 2 normally closed limit switches (S3, S4)
- Three-phase induction motor
- Flexible cable, 2.5 mm² for power and 1.5 mm² for control, in various colours
- Rails, cable trays, terminal blocks, screws

## Tools

- Wire strippers, side cutters, set of screwdrivers
- Multimeter, voltage tester

## Work sequence

1. Identify the motor windings and make the connection that suits the supply.
2. Fix the devices on the wiring board following the layout plan.
3. Wire the power circuit: check that two phases are swapped on KM2.
4. Wire the control circuit with both hold-in contacts and the electrical interlock.
5. Test with no voltage applied using the multimeter: no continuity between phases when KM1 and KM2 are operated by hand one after the other.
6. Live test in the presence of the trainer: run direction 1, stop, run direction 2, stop; check that S2 has no effect while the motor runs in direction 1.
7. Add S3 and S4, then test the end-of-travel stop by operating each limit switch by hand.

**Estimated time**: 6 hours, demonstration included.

## Assessment

- Correct position of the devices and secure mounting: 15%
- Operation of the circuit: 60%
- Compliance with standards, choice of equipment and neat work: 10%
- Practical report: 15%
