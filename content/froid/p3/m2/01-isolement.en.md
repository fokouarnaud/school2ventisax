## Target competence

The learner will be able to identify the electrical equipment used in industrial installations, state its role and describe how it works.

## Isolating devices

An isolating device is any device that can separate the installation concerned from every source of voltage.

## The isolator

The isolator (sectionneur), or disconnector, is an electromechanical device that visibly separates an electrical circuit from its supply. When open, it provides an electrically safe isolating distance.

### Role

- Open a circuit to switch off a device or a group of devices safely.
- Let you see the position of the moving parts, to make sure the isolator is really open or closed.

### Remember

- An isolator has **no breaking or making capacity**.
- An isolator must **never be opened on load**.

The three-pole fuse switch-disconnector Q1 combines three power contacts (terminals 1-2, 3-4, 5-6), each carrying a fuse, and early-break contacts (13-14, 23-24). Because they open before the power contacts, the early-break contacts cut the control circuit: the contactors drop out and the current is cut before the isolator opens.

## The switch

A switch (interrupteur) has a breaking and making capacity. It is used to:

- put the installation into service;
- shut the installation down;
- separate the installation from every source of voltage.

A four-pole switch cuts the three phases and the neutral: L1, L2, L3 and N come in on terminals 1, 3, 5, 7 and leave by terminals 2, 4, 6, 8.
