## Practical activities

**Task**: identify all the electrical equipment used in industrial installations.

## Equipment

- The equipment in the store and the workshop: fuse switch-disconnector, switch, fuses, thermal-magnetic circuit breaker and RCD, contactors, thermal overload relay, timer relay, coil-and-contact relays, push buttons, indicator lamps, terminal blocks
- The summary table of equipment from this module

## Tools

- Multimeter (ohmmeter or continuity test)
- Set of screwdrivers

## Work sequence

1. For each device shown, note its family, its function and its circuit code (Q, F, KM, S, H…).
2. Note the information marked on the device: rating, curve, breaking capacity, coil voltage, setting range.
3. Identify the power and control terminals (1-2, 3-4, 5-6, 13-14, 21-22, 95-96, 97-98, A1-A2).
4. With no voltage applied, use the ohmmeter to check the contacts of a contactor at rest, then while pressing its moving part.
5. Draw the symbol of each device and fill in an identification sheet.

**Estimated time**: 2 hours.

## Assessment criteria

- Correct identification (family, function, code)
- Correct identification of the terminals
- Correct symbols
- Quality of the identification sheet
