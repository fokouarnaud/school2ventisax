## Practical activities

**Task**: identify the two windings of a part-winding motor, then wire and test its two-step start.

## Equipment list (for 18 workstations)

| Item | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Motor contactor | 36 |
| Control contactor | 18 |
| Thermal overload relay | 36 |
| On-delay timer block | 18 |
| Four-pole isolator | 18 |
| Circuit breaker | 18 |
| Terminal block | 108 |
| Start, stop and emergency stop push buttons | 18 |
| Rails, cable tray, screws | As per layout |
| Cable 4 mm² or 2.5 mm² for power | As per layout |
| Cable 1.5 mm² for control | As per layout |

## Tools

- Multimeter (ohmmeter), voltage tester
- Wire strippers, side cutters, set of screwdrivers

## Work sequence

1. With no voltage applied, measure with the ohmmeter the resistances between terminals 1-2, 2-3, 3-1, then 4-5, 5-6, 6-4; work out the motor type (50/50 or 66/33) and which is the first winding.
2. Remove any link from the terminal board (case H).
3. Fix the devices on the wiring board following the layout plan.
4. Wire the power circuit: K1 and F1 to 1U, 1V, 1W; K2 and F2 to 2U, 2V, 2W, in the same phase order.
5. Wire the control circuit: protections in series, start, stop, emergency stop, K1 hold-in contact, K2 delay set to 1 s at most.
6. Test with no voltage, then live in the presence of the trainer: check that K2 pulls in after the delay and that both windings turn the same way (steady current on the clamp meter).

**Estimated time**: 6 hours, demonstration included.

## Assessment

- Correct identification and position of the devices, secure mounting: 15%
- Operation of the circuit: 60%
- Compliance with standards, choice of equipment and neat work: 10%
- Practical report: 15%
