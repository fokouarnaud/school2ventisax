## Target competence

The learner will be able to explain part-winding starting, identify the windings of a part-winding motor and wire its starter.

## General

A motor started direct-on-line draws a high inrush current, about 3 to 8 times its rated current. A part-winding motor (démarrage fractionné) reduces this peak, because its two windings are switched on in two steps.

Part-winding starting is meant for special motors that have **two electrically independent windings**, each connected in star:

- either exactly equal in power: **50% / 50%**;
- or of different powers: **two thirds / one third** (66% / 33%).

Together the two windings make one large motor, but they are switched on one after the other.

## Role

The purpose of this arrangement is to limit the starting current of a **high-power compressor**. It is very common in industrial refrigeration and air conditioning.

## Operation

1. **First step**: the motor starts with only the first winding supplied.
2. **Second step**: after a very short delay (**1 s at most**), the second winding is supplied as well.

Note that the motor can be damaged if it runs on the starting winding alone (first step) without moving on to the whole winding (second step).

Because the two windings are independent, so is their wiring. To avoid trouble, make sure they are wired so that, once switched on, **they turn in the same direction**.
