## Practical activities (assignments)

1. Find the Daikin, Samsung and LG air-conditioner catalogues online and make a selection.
2. Find the specifications of some equipment online from its rating plate or model.
3. Download texts and translate them.
4. Find the name of each piece of workshop equipment and apparatus online (Google search by text, sound, photo or description).
5. Explore professional websites and other search engines.

The programme includes guided research in the computer room and writing a summary report of the results.

**Assessment**: practical test on searching for and using data.
