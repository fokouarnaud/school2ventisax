## Target skills

- Download and use catalogues.
- Determine compressor power from the rating plate.
- Translate any language into French.
- Research equipment.

The learner will be able to use the internet as a professional tool to search for, translate and use technical information.

## Introduction

The internet is a set of interconnected global networks that lets computers and servers communicate efficiently using a common communication protocol (IP).

With the rapid development of information and communication technology on the one hand, and the constant creation of varied (model, brand, manufacturer…) and increasingly sophisticated technical equipment on the other, technicians and professionals in the trade rely on the internet for certain everyday tasks.

## Module content

- The role of the internet in training and maintenance.
- Practical exercises: finding catalogues, identifying equipment, translating documents.
