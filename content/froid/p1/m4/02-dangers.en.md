## What are the dangers?

Dangerous chemical fumes, lack of oxygen, risk of fire, but also flooding, drowning and asphyxiation by dust or grain.

## What should be done?

- Avoid working in confined spaces whenever possible.
- Otherwise, assess the risks and plan how to control them: ventilate, close supply valves, provide breathing apparatus if oxygen is short.
- Plan emergency arrangements: know whether people are all right and be able to get them out.
- Never let anyone enter without being sure the space is safe.
