## Integration activities

1. Recognise and correctly wear PPE and equipment for work at height (ladders, stepladders, harnesses…).
2. Practise working at height on scaffolding or a platform built on site.
3. Carry out electrical work in a ceiling void or a stairwell.
4. Run an assessment towards the learners' clearance certification, based on how well they have acquired the skills.

The programme also includes a case study of a confined-space accident and a simulated electrical lock-out.

**Assessment**: multiple-choice test and a practical risk-identification exercise.
