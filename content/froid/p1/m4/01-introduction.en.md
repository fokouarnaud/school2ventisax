## What is a confined space?

A confined space is a place that is fully or largely enclosed and that exposes workers to a likely risk of fire, explosion, loss of consciousness, asphyxiation or drowning.

It can be small and narrow, such as a pipe or a tank, or huge, such as a grain silo holding several hundred cubic metres.

## Module content

- Defining confined spaces: risks of asphyxiation, fire, poisoning.
- Preventive measures: ventilation, detection, evacuation plan.
- Electrical clearance: classification of levels (B0, H0, BR, BC, etc.).
