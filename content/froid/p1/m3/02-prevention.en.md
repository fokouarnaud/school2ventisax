## Preventive measures

The employer first assesses the risks: the height, how long and how often the task lasts, and the condition of the work surface.

Before starting work at height:

- avoid working at height whenever possible;
- otherwise, prevent falls with an already safe workplace or suitable equipment;
- minimise the height and the consequences of a fall when the risk cannot be removed;
- always favour measures that protect everyone (collective protection) over measures that only protect the worker concerned (personal protection).
