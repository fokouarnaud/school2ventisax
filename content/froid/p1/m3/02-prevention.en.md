## Preventive measures

The employer first assesses the risks. The height, how long and how often the task lasts, and the condition of the work surface are all factors to take into account.

Before starting work at height, the employer follows these simple steps:

- avoid working at height whenever possible;
- when it is hard to avoid, prevent falls by using either an already safe workplace or suitable equipment;
- minimise the height and the consequences of a fall, with suitable equipment, when the risks cannot be removed;
- for each measure, always favour measures that protect everyone (collective protection) over those that only protect the worker concerned (personal protection).
