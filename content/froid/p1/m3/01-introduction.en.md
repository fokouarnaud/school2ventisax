## Introduction

Working at height remains one of the main causes of death and serious injury: falls from roofs or ladders, and falls through fragile surfaces.

Working at height means working in a place where, if no precautions are taken, a person could fall far enough to be injured (for example through a fragile roof, down a lift shaft or an unprotected staircase).

## Case study

The employer must make sure the work is properly planned, supervised and carried out by competent people who use suitable equipment for working at height.
