## Introduction

Working at height remains one of the main causes of death and serious injury. Among the most common cases: falls from roofs or ladders and falls through fragile surfaces.

Working at height means working in a place where, if no precautions are taken, a person could fall far enough to be injured (for example through a fragile roof, down a lift shaft or an unprotected staircase).

This module shows how employers can take simple, practical steps to reduce the risk of falls for employees working at height.

## Case study: what should the employer do?

The employer must make sure the work is properly planned, supervised and carried out by competent people, meaning people with the skills, knowledge and experience needed to do it safely. These people must use suitable equipment for working at height.

The employer takes a reasonable approach to the precautions needed: simple, low-risk tasks need less planning, and in some low-risk situations common sense is enough.
