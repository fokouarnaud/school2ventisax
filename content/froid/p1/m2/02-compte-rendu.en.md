## The practical work report

Practical exercises are designed in three phases: preparation, launch and follow-up.

A practical work report gives information on:

- the technical object being studied;
- the skill expected at the end of the task;
- the tools and equipment needed for the task;
- the method used or the different steps of the task;
- diagrams, where relevant;
- the time taken;
- the difficulties met during the exercise;
- the assessment criteria.

Each of these points must be developed with detailed explanations.

## Practical activities (assignments)

- Simulate a practical exercise in the workshop.
- Write a practical work report with guidance.
- Assess the learners' ability to write practical work reports, in pairs.
