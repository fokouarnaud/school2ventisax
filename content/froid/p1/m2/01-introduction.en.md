## Introduction

Learning, in general and as practised in the new curricula, happens in stages:

- acquiring theoretical knowledge;
- acquiring practical knowledge;
- the hands-on phase carried out by the learner.

The first two stages present what already exists: how to make or repair the technical object being taught.

The third stage, devoted to hands-on work or application exercises, is the practical phase in which learners develop their dexterity or their research skills. Practical work is a set of activities or tasks supervised by the teacher to help learners acquire one or more concrete skills. At the end of every practical exercise, it is recommended to write a detailed report.

## Types of reports

There are several types of reports depending on the learning activity:

- the practical work report;
- the activity report;
- the servicing or maintenance report;
- the internship report.

## Module content

- Types of reports (practical work, activity, internship)
- Writing method: introduction, body, conclusion
- Quality criteria for a report: precision, concision, methodological rigour
- A sample internship report structure

**Assessment**: written report, marked and graded (individual and group).
