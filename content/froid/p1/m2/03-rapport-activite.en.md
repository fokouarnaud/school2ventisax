## The activity report

During training, learners carry out activities off site, such as building-site or company visits. At the end of a company visit, it is recommended to produce an activity report, or visit report, giving information on:

- the date and time;
- the site or place: full name and address;
- the objectives set at the start of the visit;
- the human and material resources;
- the method (the different stages of the visit);
- the guides (academic and/or professional supervisors);
- the learning activities developed or deepened during the visit;
- the theoretical and/or practical points highlighted;
- the difficulties met in the field;
- the recommended solutions;
- the evaluation points.

Each of these points must be developed with detailed explanations.

## The periodic activity summary

Activity summaries are also commonly drawn up after a set period (daily, weekly, monthly…). This report gives information on:

- the number of jobs;
- the type of jobs;
- the average downtime;
- the cost of the various jobs;
- the resources used (equipment and staff);
- the job reports;
- the difficulties met;
- suggestions and recommendations for improvement.

This type of report is used for the overall analysis of a company, to build decision-making tools.

## Practical activities (assignments)

- Simulate a company visit, in this case to LA VOCATION SERVICES.
- Write an activity report with guidance.
- Assess the learners' ability to write an activity report.
