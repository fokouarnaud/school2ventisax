## General introduction

The training offered by the LA VOCATION vocational training centre aims to give learners the technical, methodological and behavioural skills they need to enter the working world effectively. It develops safety, rigour and autonomy in carrying out technical work, while building a culture of prevention and quality.

## How the training is organised

- **Total duration**: 48 hours across 6 modules.
- **Teaching methods**: theory sessions, case studies, practical work, simulations.
- **Assessments**: guided exercises, multiple-choice tests, written reports, real-life scenarios.
- **Target audience**: trainees in technical and vocational training (refrigeration technicians, electricians, maintenance technicians, etc.).

## The modules

| Module | Duration | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Safety at work | 8 h | Multiple-choice test and observation of safe practices during a workshop |
| 2. Report writing techniques | 16 h | Written report, marked and graded (individual and group) |
| 3. Working at height | 4 h | Observation of safe practices in the workshop |
| 4. Work in confined spaces and electrical clearance | 4 h | Multiple-choice test and a practical risk-identification exercise |
| 5. Using a maintenance sheet | 8 h | Correctly filling in a service sheet after a practical exercise |
| 6. Internet research | 8 h | Practical test on searching for and using data |

## Assessment methods

- **Formative assessment**: exercises and simulations in every module.
- **Summative assessment**: a final exam combining a multiple-choice test, a case study and a written report.
- **Certification**: awarded to learners who pass every module.
