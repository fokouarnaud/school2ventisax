## Why safety at work matters

The training offered by the LA VOCATION vocational training centre aims to give learners the technical, methodological and behavioural skills they need to enter the working world effectively.

It develops **safety, rigour and autonomy** in carrying out technical work, while building a culture of prevention and quality.

## What this module covers

- Electrical safety: electric shock, electrocution, danger thresholds, basic rules
- Safety around pressurised equipment: gas, steam, liquids, vacuum
- Case studies of real accidents
