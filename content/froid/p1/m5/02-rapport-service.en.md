## The service report (maintenance sheet)

The LA VOCATION centre uses a bilingual service report (Dutch and French), numbered (S…). The type of job is ticked: **maintenance**, **breakdown** or **repair**.

**Header**

- Customer, job number, building, unit number.
- Work order, date, technician.
- Outdoor temperature, supply voltage, control voltage.
- Type of refrigerant (R…).
- Make, type and serial number of the unit.

**Checkpoints** (ticked "OK" or "not OK")

- **Fans** (evaporator and condenser): current per phase (L1, L2, L3), air filter, cleanliness, direction of rotation, belt, mounting, motor, balance, electrical connection.
- **Evaporator**: water or air inlet and outlet temperatures, water differential pressure switch, flow switch, condensate tray, water drain, temperature setpoint, water leaks.
- **Compressor**: oil sample, crankcase heater, oil level, valves, klixon (thermal protection), capacity control, suction (LP) and discharge (HP) pressures, oil pressure, running hours, number of starts, current per phase, insulation resistance, discharge, liquid and suction temperatures, subcooling and superheat.
- **Pump and condenser**: current, rotation, mounting, electrical connection, water filter, bleeding, water pressure, scaling, air flow, leaks, cleanliness, glycol (antifreeze).
- **Humidifier and heating**: fill and drain valves, electrodes, heating elements, klixon.
- **Controls**: LP, HP and oil pressure switches, fan control, contactors, time relays, thermal protection, fuses, control and frost thermostats, liquid injection.
- **Refrigeration circuit**: non-return valve, shut-off valves, solenoid valve, sight glass, filter drier, hot-gas bypass, thermostatic expansion valve, leak test, refrigerant charge.

**Footer**: technician's certificate number, leak detector used (minimum sensitivity 5 ppm, type, serial number, calibration date), quantities of refrigerant added or recovered, total system charge, logbook entry, test pressure, customer's name and signature.
