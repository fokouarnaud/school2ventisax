## The work sheet

The work sheet (bilingual Dutch and French) summarises the job. It records:

- the customer, building, job number, date and work order;
- the type of job: maintenance, breakdown or repair;
- the unit concerned and the attachments (service report no. S…, leak and pressure test no. P…);
- the work done;
- the start and end time of the work;
- the equipment and consumables used;
- what remains to be done or ordered;
- remarks;
- the final status: **ready** or **not ready**;
- the technician's name, and the customer's name and signature.

Example: cleaning the evaporator filter, with replacing the thermostat noted under "still to do".
