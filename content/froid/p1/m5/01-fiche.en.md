## What is a service sheet?

A service sheet is an essential document for mobile workers: it is the proof and the summary of a job. The report can be given to the customer, and it can also be scanned or photocopied so the company keeps a copy and files the work.

## Why use a service sheet?

It records the actions carried out at the customer's site. Everything can go on it: the details of the request, explanations about the job, and also any faults found and the equipment used.

The customer can then be asked to sign, to confirm they approve the work and the description of the actions. This signature is very useful when another department has to invoice the job. It confirms that the technician and the customer met and that the customer accepts the work was done.
