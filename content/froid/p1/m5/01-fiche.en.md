## What is a service sheet?

A service sheet is an essential document for mobile workers: it is the proof and the summary of a job. It can be given to the customer, and a copy is filed by the company.

## Why use one?

It records everything done at the customer's site: the details of the request, explanations about the job, any faults found and the equipment used.

The customer's signature confirms that the technician and the customer met and that the customer approves the work done. It is also very useful to the billing department.
