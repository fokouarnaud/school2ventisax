## The leak detection, pressure and vacuum test certificate

This certificate (no. P…) goes with any work on the refrigeration circuit. It lists the customer, job, building, work order, date, technician and their certification number.

## Vacuum test

| Check | Value |
|---|---|
| Minimum vacuum | ≤ 270 Pa |
| Minimum vacuum time, refrigerant charge up to 10 kg | 0.5 h |
| Minimum vacuum time, charge from 10 to 200 kg | 1 h |
| Minimum vacuum time, charge above 200 kg | 2 h |

Record the vacuum reached (bar or MPa) and the test duration. The leak check takes place after the system has run normally, or has been pressurised with its refrigerant.

## Pressure test (standard NBN E 378-2)

| Reference | Values |
|---|---|
| Ambient temperature | ≤ 32 °C / ≤ 43 °C |
| High-pressure side with condenser | 55 °C / 63 °C |
| High-pressure side with water-cooled condenser | 43 °C / 43 °C |
| Low-pressure side | 32 °C / 43 °C |
| Leak test pressure | ≤ 1 × PS |
| High-pressure switch setting | ≤ 0.9 × PS |
| Safety valve setting | ≤ 1.1 × PS |

(PS: the system's maximum allowable pressure.)

Record the low and high-side test pressures and the test duration, and answer yes or no:

- was the system checked for leaks and deformation during the test?
- was the tightness test done with a visual and foam check?
- were the safety valves checked?
- is the system free of leaks and deformation?

## Leak test

Electronic detector with a minimum sensitivity of 5 ppm: type, serial number, calibration date, refrigerant type, low and high-side pressures, and conclusion: **the system is leak-tight** (yes or no). There is space for remarks.
