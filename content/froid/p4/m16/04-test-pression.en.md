The pressure test (<span lang="fr">test de pression</span>) checks that a new or repaired circuit withstands pressure, before the leak test. It follows EN 378 and the manufacturer's instructions, which set the maximum allowable pressure PS on each side of the circuit.

**Safety**

- Dry nitrogen only, never oxygen or compressed air: oxygen in contact with oil can explode.
- The cylinder is never connected without a regulator, set before opening to the circuit.
- Isolate parts that cannot withstand the test pressure (pressure switches, relief valves, vacuum gauge) or that are rated for a lower pressure.
- Nobody in line with fittings and caps during pressurisation; area marked off.

**Procedure**

1. Connect the nitrogen cylinder, its regulator and the manifold.
2. Raise the pressure in stages, watching the gauge, up to the strength test pressure (in practice 1.1 × PS for a nitrogen test, unless otherwise specified).
3. Hold for the specified time, then reduce to the leak test pressure, at most PS.
4. Record pressure and temperature, then check every joint for leaks.
5. Write the test report: date, system, pressures, durations, temperatures, result.
6. Vent the nitrogen outdoors, then go on to evacuation.
