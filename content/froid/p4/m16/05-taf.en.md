## Equipment and tools (per workstation)

- 1 nitrogen cylinder with regulator, 1 manifold with service hoses
- 1 vacuum gauge, 1 vacuum pump
- 1 electronic detector, leak detection foam, 1 thermometer
- 1 training rig and 1 existing system

## Sequence of work (6 h)

1. Activity 1: identify the leak testing equipment (nitrogen cylinder, vacuum gauge, vacuum pump, electronic detectors, foam, manifold, thermometer).
2. Activity 2: check that the instruments suit the refrigerants and materials.
3. Activity 3: work out the leak rate of a 0.5 m³ system at 25 °C (298 K) whose nitrogen pressure drops 20 mbar in 24 hours. Expected result: 2000 × 0.5 × 0.102 / (8.314 × 298) = 0.041 kg per day, about 15 kg/year of R134a.
4. Activity 4: nitrogen pressure and leak test on the rig, with a test report.
5. Activity 5: leak search on an existing system.
6. Assessment.

## Assessment

- Accuracy and soundness of connections: 15%
- Operation (test carried out, correct calculation, leaks found): 60%
- Standards, choice of equipment and safety: 10%
- Work report and test report: 15%
