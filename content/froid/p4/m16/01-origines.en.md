**Prerequisites**: the basic refrigeration circuit, the fitter's tools, the materials used in refrigeration systems.

Refrigerants are essential to refrigeration and air conditioning systems. Refrigerant leaks, especially of hydrofluorocarbons, have a direct impact on global warming, and an indirect one too, since the system's energy efficiency drops. Good tightness is therefore essential to a system's quality, for technical, economic and regulatory reasons. Leak testing (<span lang="fr">test d'étanchéité</span>) finds leaks early and keeps large amounts of refrigerant from escaping.

## Where leaks come from

- Vibration.
- Changes in temperature and pressure, causing expansion or stress.
- Wear.
- Poor choice of components.
- Poor quality control.
- Badly controlled work on the circuit.

Apart from accidents, most leaks are at joints. They are usually small, but they can also be intermittent, which makes them harder to locate.
