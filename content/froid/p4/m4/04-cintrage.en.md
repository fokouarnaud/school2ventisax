Bending shapes the tube without a fitting, so without a brazed joint or extra risk of a leak.

1. Check that the tube is annealed, or anneal it if it is hard-drawn.
2. Choose the bender former that matches the tube diameter.
3. Work out the developed length, then mark the start of the bend on the tube.
4. Place the tube in the groove of the former, with the mark on the bender's starting point.
5. Close the hook and lower the lever slowly and steadily to the required angle, without passing the former's graduation.
6. Check the angle with a square or template, then check that the section is neither flattened nor kinked.

A flattened tube restricts the flow and creates a pressure drop: it must be replaced.
