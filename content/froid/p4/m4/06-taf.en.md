## Equipment and tools (per workstation)

- Annealed copper tube 3/8, steel tube 3/4
- 1 tube cutter, 1 deburrer, 1 flaring tool, 1 swaging tool, 1 tube bender
- 1 hacksaw, 1 vice, 1 die stock and lubricant
- Pipe clips, 1 square, 1 tape measure
- Safety glasses and gloves

## Sequence of work (12 h)

1. Trainer demonstrates the actions.
2. Work out the developed length of a 3/8" tube bent at 90°, with R = 46.5 mm and dimensions 540 mm and 298 mm. Expected result: about 818 mm.
3. Cut, deburr and bend the tube, then check the dimensions.
4. Make a bevel and a branch on the previous piece.
5. Make a sound flare and a swage.
6. Cut an outside thread on a steel tube.
7. Fix the work with clips.
8. Assessment.

## Assessment

- Accuracy of position and soundness of fixings: 15%
- Operation (dimensions, shape, conformity of parts): 60%
- Standards, choice of equipment and safety: 10%
- Work report: 15%
