Steel tube may be supplied unprotected (black tube for heating), galvanised (coated with zinc inside and out, for plumbing), or threaded at the ends with plain couplings. Lengths range from 4 to 7.50 m; the most common commercial length is 6.40 m (within 5 cm).

## Cutting

Cutting means dividing a part into two or more pieces in various shapes and angles. Measuring is done with a tape.

| Method | Tool | Result and advice |
|---|---|---|
| Chip cutting | Hacksaw (frame, steel blade hardened on one third), blade tube cutter | Clean cut, burr-free with a blade tube cutter |
| Shearing | Tube cutter with one wheel and two rollers, with three wheels (works by half-turns), chain cutter for large diameters | Clean outside, inner burr; with a chain, take care with setting up, risk of a skewed cut |
| Abrasion | Fixed or portable cut-off saw | The disc cuts by friction; clean, quick cut with few burrs |
| Fusion | Oxy-fuel cutter | Metal heated to red is cut by a jet of oxygen |

**Precautions**

- **Tube cutter**: use wheels and blades in good condition, do not lever with the cutter at the end of the cut, lubricate while cutting.
- **Cut-off saw**: do not work without guard and goggles, do not touch the cut part (hot and sharp), watch the direction of the sparks, do not put the saw down before the disc has stopped.
- **Oxy-fuel cutter**: same precautions as for a torch (see the torch module).
- **Hacksaw**: slacken the blade after use; teeth point forward when fitted; cut at 60 strokes per minute, with moderate pressure forward and none on the return, over the whole length of the blade. Use 6 teeth per centimetre for soft metals, 8 or 12 for hard or thin metals.

## Threading

Threading makes helical threads on a tube or screw, to screw a part on or seal between two parts.

- **Thread** (<span lang="fr">filetage</span>): helical groove cut in the outside of a cylindrical part.
- **Tapped thread** (<span lang="fr">taraudage</span>): helical groove cut in the inner wall of a hollow part.
- **Pitch**: distance between two consecutive thread crests. With each complete turn, the screw or tube advances by one pitch.

A tube is threaded with a die stock, manual or electric. A die stock with an interchangeable head has an inner ratchet worked by a button to select the direction of rotation; the chasers are held in a head; threading takes a single pass. Once finished, turn it back to free the head, with great care not to damage the chasers.

**How to thread**: fix the tube in the vice, fit the die of the matching size, fit the stock on the tube, lubricate the die and select the threading direction. Always lubricate while threading.

## Installation

Join by threading and tapping: straight joints (couplings, nipples, flanges), changes of direction (elbows), branches (tees, crosses, distributors), special fittings (plugs, nuts, mixed unions). The joint must withstand tightening, expansion, mechanical stress and corrosion. It can be dismantled with right and left-hand couplings or unions, and is permanent with straight couplings, elbows and tees.
