| Job | Principle and precautions |
|---|---|
| Reading HP and BP pressures and temperatures, running and stopped | Manifold valves closed; superheat and subcooling are also checked from the various temperatures |
| Charging and topping up | Putting refrigerant into a system, weighing it on scales; a zeotropic blend is charged as liquid |
| Evacuation | Removing air and moisture from the circuit; the vacuum reached is checked on the vacuum gauge (<span lang="fr">vacuomètre</span>) |
| Nitrogen pressurisation | Pressurising the circuit to look for leaks; dry nitrogen, always through a regulator, without exceeding the circuit's maximum allowable pressure PS |
| Refrigerant recovery | Removing refrigerant from a system with a recovery unit into a recovery cylinder; refrigerant must never be released to the atmosphere |
| Zeroing the manifold | Bringing the gauge needle back to zero before use |

Evacuation and charging are covered in detail in the modules on evacuation and charging, with their interactive figures.
