## Equipment and tools (per workstation)

- 1 working refrigeration rig with service valves
- 1 mechanical manifold and, if possible, 1 electronic manifold
- 1 vacuum pump, 1 vacuum gauge, 1 nitrogen cylinder with regulator
- 1 recovery unit and 1 recovery cylinder, 1 set of scales
- Safety glasses and gloves

## Sequence of work (6 h)

1. Zero the manifold.
2. Connect the hoses and read pressures stopped, then running; work out the superheat.
3. Recover the rig's refrigerant into the weighed recovery cylinder.
4. Pressurise with nitrogen and look for leaks.
5. Evacuate to the target value.
6. Recharge on the scales and restart.
7. Assessment.

## Assessment

- Accuracy and soundness of connections: 15%
- Operation (each job successful): 60%
- Standards, choice of equipment and safety: 10%
- Work report: 15%
