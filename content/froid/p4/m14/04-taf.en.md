## Equipment and tools (per workstation)

- 1 rig with a compressor oil valve and a moisture-indicating sight glass
- 1 acid test kit, 1 oil moisture test kit
- Clean, dry bottles and labels
- 1 manifold, 1 vacuum pump, compatible new oil
- Gloves and goggles

## Sequence of work (6 h)

1. Read the moisture-indicating sight glass after half an hour's running.
2. Lock off and isolate the compressor.
3. Take an oil sample in a clean, dry bottle.
4. Carry out the acid test and moisture test following the kit instructions.
5. Top up the oil, evacuate, restart.
6. Write the diagnosis and the action to take.
7. Assessment.

## Assessment

- Accuracy of sampling and cleanliness of the bottle: 15%
- Operation (tests done, accurate diagnosis): 60%
- Standards, choice of equipment and safety: 10%
- Work report: 15%
