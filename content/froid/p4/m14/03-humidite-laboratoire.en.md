## The moisture test

Moisture is checked in two ways:

- **with a moisture-indicating sight glass** on the liquid line: its indicator changes colour with the moisture in the refrigerant ("dry" and "wet" colours shown on the glass); read after at least half an hour of steady running;
- **with an oil moisture test kit**, on a sample taken as for the acid test.

## Laboratory analysis

The sample, in a clean, dry, labelled bottle (system, refrigerant, oil, date), is sent to a laboratory, which usually measures:

| Measurement | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Acid number | Oil breakdown, risk to the windings |
| Water content (ppm) | Moisture in the circuit |
| Wear metals | Wear of compressor parts |
| Viscosity | Dilution by refrigerant or wrong oil |

## What if the test is bad?

- Replace the filter drier, with an acid-removing type if the oil is acidic.
- Drain and replace the oil.
- Pull a deep vacuum, with a nitrogen break, to remove moisture.
- Test again after a few hours' running (<span lang="fr">fonctionnement</span>).
