To prevent a costly breakdown, which sometimes means a shutdown that always harms the customer, a periodic oil analysis is recommended as a preventive test. A quick on-site acid test can be done, or a sample taken for laboratory analysis. The operation has two stages: sampling, then analysis.

## Sampling

Only a sample is taken, not all the compressor oil.

1. Isolate and lock off the compressor electrically.
2. Isolate it from the rest of the circuit with its valves, or recover the refrigerant.
3. Take the sample through the oil valve, or failing that through a hose to the bottom of the crankcase (for example via the oil sight glass), into a clean, dry bottle, capped at once.
4. Close up, taking care with the seal, top up the oil, evacuate and restart.

## Quick on-site analysis

The kit contains bottles of solvent and neutraliser which, mixed with the test oil, show its condition by their colour. The technician compares the colour with the kit's instructions, which give the degree of acidity. In general:

| Colour | Result |
|---|---|
| Violet | Acidity correct |
| Yellow | High acidity |

Colours vary from kit to kit: always refer to its instructions (<span lang="fr">notice</span>).
