## Equipment and tools (per workstation)

- 1 arc welding set, electrode holder and earth clamp
- 2.5 and 3.2 mm covered electrodes
- 6 mm steel flats, 1 grinder, 1 wire brush, 1 chipping hammer
- Helmet with filter shade, gloves, apron, safety glasses, screens, extinguisher

## Sequence of work (6 h)

1. Trainer demonstration.
2. Work out the current for 2.5 mm and 3.2 mm electrodes and set the machine.
3. Prepare and bevel two flats, then tack them and check the assembly.
4. Run a flat bead and a fillet weld.
5. Clean the beads and identify any defects.
6. Assessment.

## Assessment

- Accuracy of preparation and tacking: 15%
- Operation (quality of the beads): 60%
- Standards, choice of equipment and safety: 10%
- Work report: 15%
