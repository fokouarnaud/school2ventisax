## Equipment and tools (per workstation)

- 1 evacuated rig with a rating plate showing the charge
- 1 dip-tube refrigerant cylinder, 1 set of electronic scales
- 1 manifold, 1 contact thermometer, the refrigerant's pressure-temperature table
- Gloves and safety glasses

## Sequence of work (6 h)

1. Read the plate and note the mass to charge.
2. Trainer demonstration.
3. Charge the rig to the exact mass, as liquid for a zeotropic blend.
4. Start up, record BP pressure, evaporating temperature and evaporator outlet temperature.
5. Work out the superheat and compare it with the manufacturer's value.
6. Isolate the cylinder, remove the hoses and refit the nuts.
7. Assessment.

## Assessment

- Accuracy and soundness of connections: 15%
- Operation (exact mass, correct superheat): 60%
- Standards, choice of equipment and safety: 10%
- Work report: 15%
