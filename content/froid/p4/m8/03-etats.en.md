The numbers refer to the points on the circuit diagram.

| Point | Location | State of the refrigerant | Pressure |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Compressor suction | 100% cold superheated vapour | BP |
| 2 | Compressor discharge | 100% hot superheated vapour | HP |
| 3 | Condenser inlet | Hot vapour | HP |
| 3 to 4 | Superheat removal | 100% hot vapour | HP |
| 4 to 5 | Condensation | Liquid and vapour | HP |
| 5 to 6 | Subcooling | Liquid | HP |
| 6 | Condenser outlet | 100% subcooled liquid | HP |
| 7 | Expansion valve inlet | 100% subcooled liquid | HP |
| 8 | Expansion valve outlet | Cold liquid (80% liquid, 20% vapour) | BP |
| 8 to 9 | Evaporator | Liquid and vapour: the liquid evaporates progressively | BP |
| 9 | Evaporator outlet | 100% cold vapour | BP |
