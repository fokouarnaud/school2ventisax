## Equipment and tools (per workstation)

- 1 working refrigeration rig or appliance
- 1 manifold gauge set
- 1 electronic thermometer
- Record sheets and pencil

## Sequence of work (6 h)

1. On an appliance, identify the basic components.
2. Draw the basic refrigeration circuit.
3. Identify the states of the refrigerant on a training rig.
4. Locate the high and low-pressure sides.
5. Record pressures and temperatures at the accessible points.
6. Assessment.

## Assessment

- Accuracy of the drawing and identification: 15%
- Operation of the readings: 60%
- Standards, choice of equipment and safety: 10%
- Work report: 15%
