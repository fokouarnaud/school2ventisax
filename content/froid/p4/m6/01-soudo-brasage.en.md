## General points

Braze welding (<span lang="fr">soudo-brasage</span>) is a joining process in which the joint is built up progressively, using a technique similar to fusion welding.

The melting point of the filler metal or alloy is below that of the base metal, but above 450 °C.

It is an operation between brazing and welding, on the sole condition that the edges are prepared as for an ordinary weld. The filler fills the joint progressively, without entering by capillary action.
