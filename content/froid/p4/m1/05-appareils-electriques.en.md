| Instrument | Use | Precautions |
|---|---|---|
| Digital multimeter with current clamp | Measuring AC and DC voltage, resistance and current, testing diodes, measuring frequency, capacitor value and temperature; also made without a clamp | Select the range before measuring; measure resistance only with the power off |
| Insulation tester (<span lang="fr">mégohmmètre</span>) | Measuring motor insulation resistance: it checks whether the winding insulation is still sound | Always with the power off; never on a hermetic compressor under vacuum |
| Electronic tachometer | Measuring the speed of an electric motor; the distance between the instrument and the motor is 50 mm to 200 mm | Keep clear of rotating parts |
| Phase rotation tester | Checking the phase sequence, and so the direction of rotation of a three-phase motor | Connect before commissioning a compressor with a set direction of rotation |
