| Tool | Use | Precautions |
|---|---|---|
| Oxy-acetylene welding set | Brazing copper or alloy pipes | Tinted goggles, gloves, cylinders secured, extinguisher to hand |
| Tube cutter | Cutting a copper tube to length; also made in a small size for confined work | Tighten the wheel gradually so as not to make the tube oval |
| Deburrer | Cleaning the inside of the tube after cutting | Hold the tube downwards so swarf does not fall in |
| Swaging tool (<span lang="fr">pince à emboutir</span>) | Forming a socket to join two pipes of the same diameter | Use the die for the tube diameter |
| Flaring tool (<span lang="fr">dudgeonnière</span>) | Forming conical flares at the end of pipes | Deburr first; slip the nut on before flaring |
| Tube bender | Making bends between 0 and 180° as needed | Keep to the former's radius so as not to crush the tube |
