## Equipment and tools (per workstation)

- 1 manifold gauge set, 1 vacuum pump, 1 vacuum gauge
- 1 toolbox and 1 socket set
- 1 tube cutter, 1 deburrer, 1 flaring tool, 1 swaging tool, 1 tube bender
- 1 multimeter with current clamp, 1 insulation tester
- 1 leak detector, leak detection foam, 1 set of electronic scales
- Offcuts of copper tube

## Sequence of work (6 h)

1. Trainer demonstrates each tool.
2. Identify each tool and state its use in a table.
3. Read a pressure and set the manifold valves for evacuation.
4. Cut, deburr, flare and swage an offcut of tube.
5. Measure a voltage, a resistance and a current on a training rig.
6. Assessment.

## Assessment

- Accuracy in handling and storing tools: 15%
- Operation of measurements and actions: 60%
- Standards, choice of equipment and safety: 10%
- Work report: 15%
