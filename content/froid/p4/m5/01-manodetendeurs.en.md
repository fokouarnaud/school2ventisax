Each cylinder has a pressure regulator (<span lang="fr">manodétendeur</span>) that shows the cylinder pressure (filling pressure) and sets the outlet pressure to the torch (working pressure). Its role is to expand the compressed gas in the cylinder to the working pressure for the job (pressure/flow relationship).

## General characteristics

All mixing torches work with oxygen (or compressed air) and fuel gas at constant working pressure. But pressure in cylinders and distribution lines varies and is generally too high: a regulator is needed. The gas type is marked on the regulator. It has a one-piece base with internal filter, an internal safety valve, a high-pressure gauge (cylinder pressure), a low-pressure gauge (working pressure), a high-pressure gas inlet, a low-pressure outlet and a regulating screw.

## Single-stage regulators without gauges

Used for cutting, heating, welding and medium work, with fine adjustment of the outlet pressure by the handle on the calibrated bonnet. Maximum supply pressure: 230 bar.

## Flowmeter regulator with electric heater

Supplied at 110 and 220 V, it keeps a stable temperature up to a continuous CO2 flow of 30 l/min. Maximum supply pressure: 230 bar. Power cable length: about 3 m.
