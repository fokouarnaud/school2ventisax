## Equipment and tools (per workstation)

- 1 complete oxy-acetylene set with regulators
- Assorted torch nozzles
- Lighter, tinted goggles, gloves
- Offcuts of copper sheet and tube
- Fire extinguisher to hand

## Sequence of work (6 h)

1. Trainer demonstrates starting up.
2. Connect the regulators with the screws backed off, then open the cylinders slowly.
3. Set the working pressures and purge the hoses.
4. Light the torch and adjust in turn the neutral, oxidising and carburising flames.
5. Return to the neutral flame and heat an offcut of tube.
6. Shut down the set in order.
7. Assessment.

## Assessment

- Accuracy of connections and security of cylinders: 15%
- Operation (flames set, order followed): 60%
- Standards, choice of equipment and safety: 10%
- Work report: 15%
