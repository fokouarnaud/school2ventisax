## Starting up

1. With the regulating screws backed off, connect the regulators.
2. Open the cylinder valves slowly.
3. Set the oxygen working pressure.
4. Set the acetylene working pressure, as prescribed, without exceeding the limit values.
5. Purge each hose separately, in the open air and away from any flame.
6. Open the acetylene valve on the torch, light it, then open the oxygen valve.

## Shutting down

1. Close the acetylene valve on the torch.
2. Close the oxygen valve on the torch.
3. Close the cylinder valves.
4. Purge the hoses, then back off the regulating screws.

## Welding equipment

Oxygen cylinder and acetylene cylinder secured, two regulators, blue (oxygen) and red (acetylene) hoses, torch and nozzles, tinted goggles, lighter.

## Holding the torch

The torch is tilted 50° to 70° to the work in the welding direction, and about 45° for welding the other way, with the filler rod (<span lang="fr">métal d'apport</span>) tilted about 45°.
