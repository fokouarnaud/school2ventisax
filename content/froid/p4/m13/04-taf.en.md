## Equipment and tools (per workstation)

- 1 semi-hermetic compressor with service valves on a rig
- 1 manifold, 1 vacuum pump, 1 sealed container with two connections, 1 oil syringe
- 1 can of oil compatible with the refrigerant, 1 nitrogen cylinder with regulator
- 1 recovery unit and 1 recovery cylinder
- Gloves, goggles, drip tray

## Sequence of work (6 h)

1. Read the compressor plate and choose the oil from the table.
2. Lock off, isolate the compressor and recover the refrigerant.
3. Drain the oil by vacuum and keep it for the acid test.
4. Evacuate and fill with new oil to the level.
5. Put the refrigerant back and restart.
6. Assessment.

## Assessment

- Accuracy and soundness of connections: 15%
- Operation (correct level, tightness, running): 60%
- Standards, choice of equipment and safety: 10%
- Work report: 15%
