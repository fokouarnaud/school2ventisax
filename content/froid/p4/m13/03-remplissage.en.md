Oil is charged or topped up with refrigeration oil compatible with the refrigerant: mineral and alkylbenzene oils for HCFCs, polyolester for HFCs and HFOs; for hydrocarbons and ammonia, follow the compressor manufacturer's specification. Filling can be done by gravity or with a syringe, but the vacuum method is quicker and avoids handling mistakes.

## Vacuum filling procedure

1. Connect the manifold to the compressor valves.
2. Front-seat the low-pressure valve.
3. Run the compressor, then stop it when the low pressure is between 0.3 and 0.1 bar gauge.
4. Front-seat the high-pressure valve and switch off the compressor supply.
5. Connect the vacuum pump to the manifold centre port, start it and open the manifold BP valve: evacuation takes place.
6. If the compressor has an oil charging valve at the bottom of the crankcase, connect a hose to it with the other end in the oil can, then open the valve: the oil is drawn in.
7. Check the level; once it is right, filling is finished. Let the vacuum pump run for a while.

If the compressor has no oil port, draw the oil in through the compressor BP valve, after stopping and disconnecting the vacuum pump to connect the filling hose to the manifold (<span lang="fr">manifold</span>). Once filled, evacuate the compressor.
