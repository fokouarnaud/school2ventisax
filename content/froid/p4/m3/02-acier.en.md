Steel tube is used in industrial equipment, in particular in ammonia systems, since this toxic refrigerant is very corrosive and reacts chemically with copper.

Joints are welded or screwed. Sizes in inches are 1/8, 1/4, 3/8, 1/2, 5/8, 3/4, 1, 1 1/4, 2, 2 1/2, 3...

| | Black steel tube (<span lang="fr">TAN</span>) | Galvanised steel tube (<span lang="fr">TAG</span>) |
|---|---|---|
| **Uses** | Heating systems (industrial, commercial and domestic), gas distribution | Water supply systems, fire mains |
| **Joints** | Fusion welding (torch, TIG, arc), screwing (threading and tapping) | Screwing (threading and tapping), braze welding with a torch |
