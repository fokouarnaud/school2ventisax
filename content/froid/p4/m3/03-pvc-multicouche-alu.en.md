## Multilayer tube

These are tubes of synthetic material with an aluminium core, with layers of PERT or PEX. They are for water systems only: **water only, never refrigerant**.

## PVC pipe

In air conditioning, PVC pipe is mainly used to drain condensate (water formed by the evaporator), and also to embed pipework. It is handy because it is light and fairly easy to install.

| Type | Use |
|---|---|
| PVC EP (rainwater) | Rainwater drainage |
| PVC EU (waste water) | Waste water and condensate drainage |
| Perforated PVC | Septic tank drainage fields |
| PVC-C (chlorinated PVC) | Hot water supply, aggressive fluids and liquids |

Sizes include 32, 40, 50, 63 and 100 mm. Use standard pipes, marked with conformity, pipe type, diameter and thickness (for example 40 × 3).

PVC is worked without welding, and heating it is forbidden. It needs a perfect cut and emery cloth (<span lang="fr">toile émeri</span>) for keying and deburring. Tools: a hacksaw, a mitre box, fine emery cloth.

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Mark the cut around the pipe |
| 2 | Place the pipe in the mitre box |
| 3 | Cut with the hacksaw held square to the pipe |
| 4 | Key the outside of the pipe with emery cloth where it will be glued |
| 5 | Deburr the cut |
| 6 | Chamfer the outer end of the pipe |

**Gluing**: wipe the parts with a clean lint-free cloth; degrease both parts with a compatible cleaner; once dry, apply glue carefully to both; push the pipes together in one movement; wipe off the glue and leave to dry for a few minutes.

**Advantages**: low cost, easy to transport and fit, very wide choice. **Drawback**: the rules for fitting must be well known because of its high expansion.

## Aluminium tube

Aluminium tube has many industrial uses. In refrigeration it is found in vehicle air conditioning and in ammonia (NH3) systems. It weighs only a third as much as steel. It does not tarnish, is an excellent heat conductor, resists corrosion and can be welded with a process and filler suited to aluminium.
