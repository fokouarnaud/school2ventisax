## Purpose of pipework

To carry a fluid (gas or liquid) from one item of equipment to another, or from equipment to storage (tank or receiver).

In refrigeration systems, the first purpose of pipework is to link the main components of the system: compressor, condenser, expansion valve and evaporator. The second is to support various valves and fittings: isolating valves, check valves, etc.

## Functions

Pipework must:

- withstand the service conditions (pressure and temperature);
- carry the fluid with minimum heat loss (insulation) or pressure drop;
- absorb its own expansion or contraction and withstand the resulting stresses;
- be tight;
- resist internal or ambient oxidation as well as possible.

## Copper tube

Copper tube is sold in two qualities: annealed (<span lang="fr">recuit</span>) and hard-drawn (<span lang="fr">écroui</span>). Up to 7/8" (22.225 mm), tube is annealed, polished, dehydrated and capped. It comes in coils of 15 to 30 metres wound to a diameter of 0.75 metre. Above that size, tube is hard-drawn, in straight lengths of 3 to 4 metres.

The sizes and wall thicknesses of metric tube are standardised. Tubes are designated by inside and outside diameter: 4×6; 6×8; 8×10; 10×12; 12×14; 14×16; 18×20; 20×22; 26×28; 34×36.

Inch tube is designated by outside diameter (O.D) alone, in fractions of an inch, inches, or inches and fractions. One inch is 25.4 mm.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| mm | 4.76 | 6.35 | 7.94 | 9.52 | 12.70 | 15.87 | 19.05 | 25.40 |

Copper tube is used in particular for medical gases (after special factory treatment), cold and hot water, natural gas, propane and butane, oxygen, compressed air and nitrogen, heating pipes, sprinkler systems and refrigerant lines.
