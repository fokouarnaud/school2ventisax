## Equipment and tools (per workstation)

- Tube samples: annealed and hard-drawn copper, black and galvanised steel, PVC, multilayer, aluminium
- 1 vernier caliper and 1 tape measure
- 1 hacksaw, 1 mitre box, fine emery cloth
- PVC glue and compatible cleaner
- Safety glasses and gloves

## Sequence of work (4 h)

1. Identify each tube and state its use in refrigeration.
2. Measure the diameters and convert inches to millimetres.
3. Trainer demonstrates cutting and gluing PVC.
4. Cut and assemble a PVC condensate drain line.
5. Check the flow.
6. Assessment.

## Assessment

- Accuracy of measurements and soundness of the assembly: 15%
- Operation of the drain line: 60%
- Standards, choice of equipment and safety: 10%
- Work report: 15%
