## Equipment and tools (per workstation)

- 1 single-phase hermetic compressor and 1 six-terminal three-phase motor
- 1 multimeter, 1 insulation tester
- 1 set of insulated screwdrivers, 1 lock-off padlock
- Paper and pencil for the readings

## Sequence of work (6 h)

1. Lock off the workstation and check that it is dead.
2. Trainer demonstrates the measurements.
3. Measure the three resistances of the single-phase compressor, identify C, A and P, and draw the diagram.
4. Remove the links from the three-phase motor, identify the three windings and check there is no short circuit between them.
5. Test the insulation of each terminal to earth.
6. Write the diagnosis and reconnect.
7. Assessment.

## Assessment

- Accuracy of identification and soundness of reconnection: 15%
- Operation (correct readings, accurate diagnosis): 60%
- Standards, lock-off and safety: 10%
- Work report: 15%
