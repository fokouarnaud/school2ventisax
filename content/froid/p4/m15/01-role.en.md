## Purpose

- Remove air and moisture from the refrigeration circuit.
- The circuit must not be under pressure, and leaks must have been checked beforehand.
- A circuit must never be left under vacuum: at least a pre-charge is made once the operation is finished.

## Starting conditions

The pipework is finished, leaks have been checked and the new filter drier has just been fitted.

Valves 3 and 4 are closed to protect the filter driers.

## Equipment required

- A vacuum pump.
- A ratchet spanner.
- A vacuum gauge (<span lang="fr">vacuomètre</span>): only this instrument measures the vacuum reached.
- A manifold gauge set (<span lang="fr">jeu de manifolds</span>).
