## Equipment and tools (per workstation)

- 1 training rig with service valves, new filter drier and liquid receiver
- 1 vacuum pump with clean oil
- 1 vacuum gauge with its valve
- 1 manifold gauge set with its hoses
- 1 dry nitrogen cylinder with pressure regulator
- 1 ratchet spanner
- Safety glasses and gloves

## Sequence of work (6 h)

1. Check the starting conditions: pipework finished, leaks checked, valves 3 and 4 closed.
2. Read from the water curve the pressure threshold at workshop temperature and note the target value.
3. Connection demonstrated by the trainer.
4. Connect the manifold set, the vacuum gauge and the vacuum pump, after checking the oil level.
5. Carry out a first evacuation and record the pressures.
6. Isolate the pump and watch the vacuum hold for 15 minutes.
7. Break the vacuum with dry nitrogen, then return the circuit to atmospheric pressure.
8. Carry out the final evacuation down to the target value.
9. Close the valves in order, stop the pump and make a pre-charge.
10. Write the report: pressures recorded, durations, vacuum hold.

## Assessment

- Accuracy and soundness of connections: 15%
- Operation (vacuum reached and hold): 60%
- Standards, choice of equipment and safety: 10%
- Work report: 15%
