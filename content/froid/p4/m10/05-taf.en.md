## Equipment and tools (per workstation)

Training rigs: a refrigerator with a current relay or PTC, a cold room with a voltage relay, an air conditioner with a capacitor, a unit with a three-phase motor.

For the group: 15 current relays, 15 voltage relays and 15 PTC relays with compressor, 15 start capacitors, 15 run capacitors, 15 multimeters, 15 current clamps, 2 insulation testers, 5 plugs.

## Sequence of work (6 h)

1. Lock off each rig and check that it is dead.
2. Trainer demonstrates the wiring.
3. Identify the windings, then wire the rig's relay or capacitor.
4. Check the wiring with the trainer before switching on.
5. Start up, record voltage, starting current and rated current.
6. Compare with the rating plate and write the analysis.
7. Assessment.

## Assessment

- Accuracy of position and soundness of connections: 15%
- Operation (starting, correct readings): 60%
- Standards, lock-off and safety: 10%
- Work report: 15%
