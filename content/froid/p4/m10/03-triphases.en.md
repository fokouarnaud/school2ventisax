A three-phase motor-compressor has no auxiliary winding and no start relay: its three windings create a rotating field that starts it unaided.

| Method | Principle | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Direct-on-line starting | All three phases applied at once by a contactor; high inrush current | Small and medium sizes |
| Star-delta starting | Starts in star, then changes to delta after a time delay; inrush cut to a third | Motors designed to run in delta on the supply, compressors starting unloaded |
| Part-winding starting | One winding, then the second after a time delay | Large semi-hermetic compressors |

**Direction of rotation**: many three-phase compressors, scroll compressors in particular, must turn in one direction only. Check the phase sequence with a tester (<span lang="fr">testeur d'ordre des phases</span>) before commissioning; if reversed, the compressor is noisy and does not compress.

**Connection**: check the motor connection from its rating plate and the supply voltage.
