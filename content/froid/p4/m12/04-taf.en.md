## Equipment and tools (per workstation)

- 1 system with service valves and 1 scrap refrigerator
- 1 suitable new compressor and 1 filter drier
- 1 manifold, 1 vacuum pump, 1 vacuum gauge, 1 recovery unit and 1 recovery cylinder, 1 set of scales
- 1 nitrogen cylinder with regulator, 1 oxy-acetylene set
- 1 acid test kit, 1 ratchet spanner, 1 line-tap valve
- Goggles, gloves, extinguisher

## Sequence of work (6 h)

1. Trainer demonstration.
2. Replace the compressor on the system with service valves.
3. Replace the refrigerator compressor, following the rules for flammable refrigerants.
4. Test the acidity of the oil from the removed compressors.
5. Commission and record the pressures.
6. Assessment.

## Assessment

- Accuracy and soundness of connections and brazed joints: 15%
- Operation (tightness, vacuum, charge, running): 60%
- Standards, choice of equipment and safety: 10%
- Work report: 15%
