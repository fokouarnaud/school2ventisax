Before any work, read the appliance's plate. Many refrigerators use a flammable refrigerant (R600a or R290): work in a ventilated room, with no flame or spark nearby, and only braze after recovering the refrigerant and purging the circuit with nitrogen.

1. Switch off and unplug the refrigerator.
2. Connect the manifold to the charging port on the suction line. On an appliance with no charging port, fit a line-tap valve (<span lang="fr">vanne à percer</span>) on the compressor suction line, where the charging port would be.
3. Connect the vacuum pump to the manifold and evacuate the hoses.
4. Replace the pump by a recovery unit and recover the circuit's refrigerant.
5. Disconnect the old compressor's electrical connections, remove it, test the acidity of its oil, then fit the new compressor under a nitrogen purge.
6. Replace the filter drier, to avoid blockages in the circuit.
7. Make the electrical connections and fit a gauge port on the compressor suction.
8. Connect the manifold and a nitrogen cylinder, then pressurise to the manufacturer's PS.
9. Release the nitrogen, connect a vacuum pump and gauge, then evacuate, break the vacuum and check the vacuum.
10. Weigh on electronic scales the refrigerant quantity shown on the plate and put it into the circuit.
11. Start up to check the pressure and correct operation.
12. Disconnect the hoses, cap the gauge port and put the appliance away.

Always make sure there is enough refrigeration oil in a motor-compressor before fitting it.
