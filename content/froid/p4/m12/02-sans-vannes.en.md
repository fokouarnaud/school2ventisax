1. Switch off the whole circuit and lock off.
2. Close the manifold's HP and BP hose valves, connect the vacuum pump to the centre port (yellow hose) and evacuate the hoses.
3. Connect the hoses to the gauge ports (blue BP, red HP), then open the HP and BP hose valves.
4. Connect a recovery unit and recover the refrigerant in the circuit.
5. Disconnect the electrical connections and unbraze the compressor.
6. Remove the old compressor and test the acidity of its oil.
7. Fit the new compressor by brazing under a nitrogen purge, and replace the filter drier.
8. Make the electrical connections.
9. Connect a nitrogen cylinder fitted with a regulator, pressure test at the circuit's PS and check for leaks.
10. Release the nitrogen, connect a vacuum pump and gauge, evacuate, break the vacuum, evacuate again and check the vacuum.
11. Remove the pump and gauge, connect the refrigerant cylinder and charge the specified quantity on electronic scales.
12. Start the system, check the pressures and top up if needed.
13. Carry out the leak test (indirect and direct), remove the hoses and cap the gauge ports.

Recovered refrigerant is only reused if it is not contaminated; otherwise it is sent for refrigerant reprocessing (<span lang="fr">traitement</span>).
