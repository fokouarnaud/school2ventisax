## Equipment and tools (per workstation)

- Assortment of copper, brass, steel and PVC fittings
- Annealed copper tube 1/4 and 3/8
- 1 tube cutter, 1 deburrer, 1 flaring tool
- 2 open-ended spanners and 1 torque wrench
- 1 nitrogen cylinder with regulator, leak detection foam

## Sequence of work (6 h)

1. Identify the fittings shown (material, shape, size, type).
2. Trainer demonstrates flaring and tightening.
3. Make two flares and assemble a flare joint.
4. Assemble a small test line with flare joints.
5. Pressurise with nitrogen and check with foam.
6. Assessment.

## Assessment

- Accuracy and soundness of joints: 15%
- Operation and tightness: 60%
- Standards, choice of equipment and safety: 10%
- Work report: 15%
