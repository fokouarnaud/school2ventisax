## Steel fittings

Steel fittings are also brazed or screwed, but there is less variety.

| Screwed | Welded |
|---|---|
| Coupling, reducer, nipple, elbow, union, tee, plug | Elbow, reducer, tee |

## PVC fittings

PVC fittings are for draining condensate from a system: male/female and female/female elbows (20°, 30°, 45°), Y-branches and 45° junctions. They are never suitable for carrying refrigerant.

## Mixed fittings

Mixed fittings (<span lang="fr">raccords mixtes</span>) combine two materials or two techniques, and are crimped or brazed. They are used in air conditioning and refrigeration, up to 48 bar. When brazing a socket fitting, the gap between tube and fitting is 0.1 to 0.3 mm. The joint is tested by pressurising the circuit with nitrogen.
