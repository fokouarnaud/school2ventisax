## Flameless press-fit fittings

A flameless press-fit fitting joins two tubes with a ring squeezed by a hand tool. The joint is clean, permanent and purely mechanical.

**Advantages**:

- flameless crimping, useful where there is a fire risk;
- no gas purging;
- quick and easy, with a light hand tool;
- reliable fitting and robust crimping system.

It is used in air conditioning (splits, multi-splits), industrial refrigeration equipment and heat pumps.

## General assessment

All these fittings aim to make the circuit tight. Which is used depends on the circuit being worked on and the aim.

Sealing tape, hemp and thread-locking compounds (<span lang="fr">frein-filet</span>) are only for threaded joints on water or galvanised steel pipework. They are never used on a flare joint in a refrigeration circuit: the seal there comes from contact between the two cones.
