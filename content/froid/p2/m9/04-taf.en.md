## Practical activities

**Task**: on a board or in an enclosure, wire and set a twilight switch, then a motion detector, each controlling a lamp.

## Equipment

- A 10 A circuit breaker (MCB) mounted in an enclosure
- A 230 V twilight switch with a switched-line output, and its instructions
- A 230 V PIR motion detector with a switched-line output, and its instructions
- Two lamps and their lampholders (class I luminaires)
- 1.5 mm² coloured cables (red, blue, green and yellow)
- DIN rail, cable trunking, junction boxes, connector strips and screws

## Tools

- Combination pliers, side cutters, wire strippers
- Set of screwdrivers
- Phase tester, voltage tester, multimeter
- An opaque cover to simulate darkness on the cell

## Work sequence

1. Mount the protective device, the twilight switch, the detector and the luminaires, then the trunking, following the layout drawing.
2. Wire the twilight switch according to its instructions: L and N, output L' to the lamp, return to neutral, PE to the luminaire.
3. Wire the motion detector in the same way to its own lamp.
4. Check that everything is firmly fixed and close the housings.
5. Test the installation dead with the multimeter, then live.
6. Set the threshold of the twilight switch and check that the lamp switches on when the cell is covered, then off when it is uncovered.
7. Set TIME to the minimum, then LUX and SENS; test the detection area by walking through it and note the switch-on time obtained.

**Estimated time**: 2 hours per learner after a demonstration.

## Assessment criteria

- Both installations work and the required settings are applied
- Safety rules are followed (switching off, checking the circuit is dead)
- Firm fixing
- The learner's dexterity
- The layout drawing is followed
- Neat workmanship (straight runs and cable bundles)
