## Comparing the two devices

| Criterion | Twilight switch | Motion detector |
|---|---|---|
| Detects | Ambient light level | Movement |
| Switches on | At the light threshold | After a detection |
| Usually switches off | When the light level rises | After a period with no detection |
| Main setting | Threshold in lux | TIME, LUX, SENS depending on the model |
| Example | Garden lighting at night | Corridor lighting when someone passes |

## Using the two devices together

A twilight switch and a motion detector can be combined so that the lamp only switches on when it is dark **and** movement is detected. During the day the lamp stays off; at night it switches on when someone passes, then off after the time delay.

The wiring depends on the outputs of the devices. Check that they are compatible, read the manufacturers' diagrams and check the permitted load. A relay or a contactor may be needed. Never improvise a series connection without checking the electrical characteristics.

## Common faults and remedies

| Symptom | Possible causes | Checks and remedies |
|---|---|---|
| The lamp does not switch on | No supply, wrong wiring, faulty lamp | Have the protective device, the wiring and the lamp checked |
| The lamp stays on | Wrong setting, permanent detection or faulty output | Check the detection area and the settings |
| The lamp switches on in daytime | Wrong LUX setting or function missing | Check the instructions and the setting |
| The lamp switches off too soon | TIME set too short | Increase the time delay |
| Poor detection | Wrong orientation, obstacle or unfavourable surroundings | Reposition as described in the instructions |
| Unwanted switching on | Spurious detections, changes in light or sensitivity set too high | Adjust the orientation, the threshold and the sensitivity |

## Conclusion

The twilight switch controls lighting according to the ambient light level, whereas the motion detector controls lighting after a detection. Used together, they can improve comfort and avoid wasting energy. For any connection, the manufacturer's diagram is the reference.
