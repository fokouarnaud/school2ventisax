## Target competence

This module enables learners to install and set a time switch (<span lang="fr">horloge</span>).

## Overview

A time switch, also called a timer clock, opens and closes one or two contacts according to the program chosen by the user.

## Uses

- In cold rooms, to stop refrigeration and start the defrost cycle
- For lighting fences and the surroundings of buildings
- For opening and closing motorised gates or doors

There are two kinds: **analogue** time switches (with a dial) and **digital** time switches.

## Operation

An analogue time switch has a **dial** surrounded by **segments** (tappets) that represent the time slots. Pushing the segments down or up sets the times at which the controlled equipment is switched on or off.

A small **synchronous motor**, permanently supplied by the mains (terminals L and N), turns the dial; as the segments pass, the output contact changes state.

Many time switches have a **power reserve** (internal battery charged by the mains), which keeps the time during a power cut.
