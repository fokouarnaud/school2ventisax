## Practical activities

**Task**: wire and program a time switch that controls a light, on a board or in an enclosure.

## Materials

- A 16 A circuit breaker fixed in an enclosure
- A modular analogue time switch (24 h)
- A lamp and its lampholder
- 1.5 mm² cables in colour (red, blue, green and yellow)
- Rail, trunking, terminal strips and screws

## Tools

- Combination pliers, side cutters, wire strippers
- Set of screwdrivers
- Voltage tester screwdriver, multimeter

## Sequence of work

1. Fix the time switch and the protective device on the rail, then the trunking, according to the layout drawing.
2. Wire the motor supply (L and N) and the lamp circuit on the time switch contact.
3. Check that everything is firmly fixed.
4. Test the circuit dead with the multimeter, then live.
5. Program a time slot set by the trainer, set the time and check that the light switches on and off.

**Estimated time**: 2 hours per learner after a demonstration.

## Assessment points

- The circuit works and follows the programmed time slot
- Equipment firmly fixed
- Learner's dexterity
- Layout drawing respected
- Neat work (straight runs and cable looms)
