## Analogue time switches

They are fitted in the consumer unit on a DIN rail (one or more modules wide), or inside the equipment itself.

## Some variants

- **Preset time switches**: defrost timers in household refrigerators and freezers, set in the factory (for example 6 h of cooling, 20 min of defrost).
- **Adjustable time switches**: modular or boxed, programmed by the user; some are dedicated to defrosting cold rooms.

## Digital time switches

They are programmed with buttons and a display: several programs per day, choice of weekdays, permanent override.

## Setting a 24-hour time switch

The dial makes one turn in 24 hours. The value of one division (one segment) depends on their number:

| Number of divisions | Value of one division |
|---|---|
| 24 | 1 hour |
| 48 | 30 minutes |
| 96 | 15 minutes |
| 45 | 32 minutes (1,440 min / 45) |
| 90 | 16 minutes (1,440 min / 90) |

**Example**: to light a fence from 18:00 to 22:00 with a 96-division time switch, push down 4 × 4 = **16 segments**, from 18:00 to 22:00, then set the time by turning the dial until the current time is opposite the marker.
