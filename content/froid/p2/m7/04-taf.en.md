## Practical activities

**Task**: wire a staircase timer.

## Layout

On the board: three push buttons on three sides, one lighting point, the junction box (BD) near the supply, and the timer (t) in the enclosure.

## Materials for each learner

- A staircase timer
- A bulb and a lampholder
- Three push buttons
- Cable tray and rail
- A junction box
- Four pairs of terminal strips
- Fifteen screws
- Blue cable: 80 cm
- Brown, black or red cable: 300 cm

## Sequence of work

1. Fix the equipment, rails and trunking according to the layout drawing.
2. Wire the circuit, making cable looms where needed.
3. Check that everything is firmly fixed.
4. Test the circuit dead with the measuring instruments (multimeter, voltage tester), then the power supply.
5. Test the circuit live if the dead tests are satisfactory.

**Estimated time**: 2 hours per learner after a demonstration.

## Assessment points

- The circuit works
- Equipment firmly fixed
- Learner's dexterity
- Layout drawing respected
- Neat work (straight runs and cable looms)
- Practical report
- Finding and repairing the faults introduced by the trainer
