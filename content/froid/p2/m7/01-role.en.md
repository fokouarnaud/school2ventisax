## Target competence

Learners will be able to wire a staircase timer (<span lang="fr">minuterie</span>) in an electrical installation.

## Purpose of a staircase timer

A staircase timer switches one or more light points on from one or more control points, the lights **switching off automatically** after a preset time. It suits stairs, corridors and shared areas, where lights are often left on.

There are two types:

- the **retriggerable timer** (<span lang="fr">minuterie avec effet</span>): pressing again restarts the timing;
- the **non-retriggerable timer** (<span lang="fr">minuterie sans effet</span>): pressing again during the timing has no effect.

## Symbols

| Representation | Symbol |
|---|---|
| Architectural | Push button (double circle), timer (rectangle marked t), lighting point (cross) |
| Single-line | The same devices on a single line, with the number of conductors shown by oblique strokes |
| Multi-line | Push button, time-delayed coil of the timer (A1-A2) and its contact, lamp |
