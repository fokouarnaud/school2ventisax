The course is bilingual. This glossary gives the English term used in the English version of the course, following IEC (International Electrotechnical Commission) vocabulary and British usage. The installation rules quoted come from the French standard **NF C 15-100**, which largely matches the international standard **IEC 60364**. The first time a term appears in a module, the French term is given in brackets.
