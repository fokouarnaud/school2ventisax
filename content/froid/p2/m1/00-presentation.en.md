## Course outline

This part of the course, **Basics of domestic electrical installations**, is delivered by the La Vocation vocational training centre (2025/2026 session). It has eight modules:

1. Basic electrical concepts, electrical equipment and the electrician's tools
2. Designing the electrical installation of a new house
3. One-way switching
4. Two-gang one-way switching (double switch)
5. Two-way switching
6. Impulse relay circuit
7. Staircase timer circuit
8. Installing and setting a time switch

## Expected behaviour

To demonstrate competence, learners must apply the basic principles and techniques of electricity under the conditions, criteria and details below.

## Assessment conditions

- Using the necessary components
- Using a suitable wiring board
- Using the necessary tools and instruments

## General performance criteria

- Accurate explanations of the basic principles of electricity
- Accurate calculation of electrical quantities
- Accurate drawing
- Correct use of symbols
- Compliance with electrical standards and codes
- Neat and tidy work
- Work completed within the time allowed

## Specific performance criteria

- Correct definition of the basic concepts
- Precise description of electrical power and of the relationship between voltage, current and resistance (Ohm's law)
- Appropriate conformity checks
- Correct use of the instruments that measure electrical quantities
- Accurate description of what the instruments measure and how they work
- Correct list of the quantities each instrument can measure
- Correct operating procedure: choice of function (where relevant), choice of range, connection of the probes
- Drawing an electrical circuit diagram
- Reading the instrument
