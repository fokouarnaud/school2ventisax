## Practical activities

- **Tools**: use each of the electrician's tools correctly.
- **Task 1**: identify all the electrical equipment available in the workshop stock.
- **Task 2**: explain in detail the markings on the rating plates of this equipment.
- **Task 3**: measure the capacitance of the capacitors available.
- **Task 4**: measure voltages and currents on the mains supply and on various installations.

## Equipment and tools

- Equipment from stock: circuit breakers, RCDs, fuse switch disconnector, thermal overload relay, contactor, switches, push buttons, capacitors
- Multimeter, clamp meter, capacitance meter, non-contact voltage tester
- Insulated screwdrivers, pliers

## Sequence of work

1. Sort the equipment into protective devices, control devices and loads.
2. Record and explain the markings on each rating plate (rating, voltage, breaking capacity, sensitivity, capacitance).
3. Discharge each capacitor, then measure it with the capacitance meter; compare with the value on the plate.
4. Measure a voltage with the voltmeter, then a current with the clamp meter, under the trainer's supervision.

**Estimated time**: 2 hours per learner after a demonstration.

## Assessment points

- Correct classification of the equipment
- Accurate reading of the rating plates
- Correct choice of function, range and connection of the measuring instruments
- Safety rules respected (ohmmeter and capacitance meter used on dead components)
