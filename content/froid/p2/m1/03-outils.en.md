## The electrician's tools

| Tool | Use |
|---|---|
| Cold chisel (<span lang="fr">burin</span>) | Steel tool used to cut chases and openings in walls gently |
| Centre punch (<span lang="fr">poinçon</span>) | Marks a workpiece, or pierces it |
| Club hammer (<span lang="fr">massette</span>) | Heavy hammer used to strike another tool, such as the chisel or the punch |
| Wire strippers | Remove the insulation from conductors |
| Side cutters | Cut cables |
| Long-nose pliers | Grip and place conductors in tight spaces |
| Combination pliers | Grip, tighten, twist and cut: the electrician's all-purpose pliers |
| Set of screwdrivers | Flat-blade and cross-head (Phillips) screwdrivers, the two most common types; used to fix and remove electrical accessories |
| Chalk line (<span lang="fr">cordeau de traçage</span>) | Marks straight lines to align and level equipment |
| Spirit level | Checks that equipment is fixed straight on a flat surface |
| Electric drill | Makes drilling and chases in beams and columns easier |
| Angle grinder | Makes chases in rendered walls and floors easier |
| Voltage tester screwdriver (<span lang="fr">testeur de phase</span>) | Identifies the line conductor on a live installation |

**Remember**: an electrician's screwdrivers are always insulated along the full length of the shaft.
