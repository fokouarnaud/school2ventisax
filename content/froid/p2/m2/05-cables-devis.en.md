## Stage 6: sizing the incoming cable

The cable between the Eneo meter and the consumer unit must carry the current of the main circuit breaker **and** limit the **voltage drop**. On a single-phase supply:

**ΔU = 2 × ρ × L × I / S**

with ρ = 0.0225 Ω·mm²/m for copper, L the length in metres, I the current in amperes and S the cross-section in mm². The aim is a voltage drop below 3% of 230 V, that is 6.9 V, on this link.

Example for I = 40 A and L = 30 m:

| Cross-section | Voltage drop | Percentage | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 mm² | 2 × 0.0225 × 30 × 40 / 6 = 9 V | 3.9% | Too small |
| 10 mm² | 2 × 0.0225 × 30 × 40 / 10 = 5.4 V | 2.3% | Suitable |

The longer the distance, the larger the cross-section must be.
