## Practical activities

**Task**: prepare the electrical file for the three-bedroom house studied in class.

## Materials

- Architect's plan of the house (supplied by the trainer)
- Ruler, pencil, symbol template, calculator
- Price catalogue of electrical materials

## Sequence of work

1. Mark the lighting points, socket outlets and switches on the plan, room by room, following the minimum equipment.
2. Group the points into circuits and draw the single-line diagram of the consumer unit.
3. Draw up the power schedule, apply the demand factor and deduce the rating of the main incoming circuit breaker.
4. Calculate the cross-section of the incoming cable for the distance given by the trainer.
5. Write the quotation for the materials.

**Estimated time**: 4 hours.

## Assessment points

- Minimum equipment respected in each room
- Correct grouping of circuits and correct choice of protective devices and cross-sections
- Accurate calculation of power, current and voltage drop
- Clear and tidy diagram and quotation
