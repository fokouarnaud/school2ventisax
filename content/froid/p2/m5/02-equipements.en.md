## Studying and testing the equipment

- **The double-pole circuit breaker** protects the circuit against short circuits and overloads. Its rating is chosen according to the **cross-section of the conductors** it protects: 16 A at most for 1.5 mm². Its continuity is also checked between the input and the output of each contact, in the closed position.
- **The two-way switch** controls the light point; its rated current and voltage (for example 10 A, 250 V) must suit the circuit. Continuity is checked between the common terminal and each strapper, depending on the position, making sure there is never contact between the two strappers.
- **The bulb** must match the mains voltage (230 V) and must not be blown: check the continuity of its filament with an ohmmeter, with the supply off.

## The cable

Conductors must be insulated along their whole length to avoid short circuits. The **cross-section** used is **1.5 mm²**, with four distinct colours: red and black (line and strappers), blue (neutral), green and yellow (protective earth).
