## Target competence

This module enables learners to wire a two-way switching circuit (<span lang="fr">va-et-vient</span>).

## What is two-way switching?

Two-way switching is a control circuit that switches one or more loads (bulb, chandelier) on and off from **two different control points** in the same room: both ends of a corridor, both doors of a living room.

## Operation

The circuit relies on the **two-way switch**, whose wiring is special: it has **three terminals**. One is the **common** terminal; the other two are the **strappers** (<span lang="fr">navettes</span>), which link the two switches.

- On the first switch, the common terminal receives the **line**.
- On the second, the common terminal supplies the **lamp**.
- The two strappers link the two switches.

The lamp lights when both switches connect to the **same strapper**; operating either switch changes the state of the lamp.
