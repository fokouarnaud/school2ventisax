## Practical activities

**Task**: wire a safe two-way switching circuit on a board or in an enclosure.

## Materials

- A protective fuse or circuit breaker
- Two two-way switches
- One or more lamps and their lampholders
- A line and neutral supply
- 1.5 mm² cables in colour (red, blue, green and yellow): 8 m per workstation
- A junction box; screws, rails, cable trays; cable ties (6 or 8 mm diameter), connectors or terminal strips; lacing cord for the looms

## Tools

- Combination pliers, side cutters, wire strippers, cutter knife
- Set of screwdrivers, hammer
- Voltage tester screwdriver, multimeter or clamp meter

## Sequence of work

1. Fix the equipment, rails and trunking according to the layout drawing.
2. Wire the circuit, making cable looms where needed.
3. Check that everything is firmly fixed.
4. Test the circuit dead with the measuring instruments (multimeter, voltage tester), then the power supply, and finally live if the dead tests are satisfactory.
5. Write a practical report.

**Estimated time**: 2 hours per learner after a demonstration.

## Fault-finding exercise

- The trainer introduces faults into the learners' circuits; each learner finds the fault and repairs it.
- Additional exercise with surface-mounted equipment.

## Assessment points

- The circuit works
- Equipment firmly fixed
- Learner's dexterity
- Layout drawing respected
- Neat work (straight runs and cable looms)
