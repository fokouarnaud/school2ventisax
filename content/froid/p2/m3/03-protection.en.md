## Protection and cross-section of lighting circuits

A circuit's protective device is chosen according to the **cross-section of its conductors**: it must trip before the cable overheats dangerously.

| Type of circuit | Copper cross-section | Aluminium cross-section | Fuse | Circuit breaker |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lighting | 1.5 mm² | 2.5 mm² | 10 A | 16 A |

A lighting circuit supplies **no more than 8 lighting points** (NF C 15-100, IEC 60364). The circuit is protected upstream by a 30 mA residual current device.
