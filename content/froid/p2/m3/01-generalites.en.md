- Identify the materials needed
- Explain how the circuit works
- Read a schematic diagram and describe the connections

## Overview

One-way switching controls one or more light points from **a single place**.

## Schematic diagram

**Line (L)** → **switch** → **lamp** → **neutral (N)**

The diagram is functional and simplified. The line conductor goes through the switch, while the neutral goes straight to the lamp. The protective conductor (green and yellow) is connected to the earth terminal of the light fitting, where there is one.

## Operation

- When the switch closes the circuit, current flows and the lamp lights.
- When the switch opens the circuit, the current is broken and the lamp goes out.

## Conductor colours

| Conductor | Colour | Reference |
|---|---|---|
| Line | Red, brown or black | L |
| Neutral | Blue | N |
| Protective earth | Green and yellow | PE |

**Safety rule**: the switch always breaks the **line** conductor. If it broke the neutral, the lampholder would stay live with the lamp off, and changing the bulb could give an electric shock.
