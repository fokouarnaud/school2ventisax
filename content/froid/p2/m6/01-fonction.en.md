## Target competence

Learners will be competent in wiring an impulse relay (<span lang="fr">télérupteur</span>).

## Function

An impulse relay switches one or more light points on and off from **several control points**, in practice from three or more points, where two-way switching is no longer enough.

## Principle

The impulse relay is a **bistable** relay with two circuits:

- the **control circuit**: push buttons S1, S2, S3, connected **in parallel**, supply coil K1 (terminals A1 and A2);
- the **power circuit**: contact K1 (terminals 1 and 2) supplies the lamps.

Each **pulse** on any push button changes the state of the contact: it closes if the lamp was off and opens if it was on. The contact keeps its position between two pulses. As many push buttons as needed can be added, in parallel.

## Use

In a house, impulse relays are used mainly for **corridors**, stairs and large rooms with several entrances.
