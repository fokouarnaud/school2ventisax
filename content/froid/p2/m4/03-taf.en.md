## Practical activities

**Task**: wire a safe double switch circuit on a board or in an enclosure.

## Materials

- A protective fuse or circuit breaker
- A double switch
- Two or more lamps and their lampholders
- A line and neutral supply
- 1.5 mm² cables in colour (red, blue, green and yellow)
- A junction box, screws, cable ties (6 or 8 mm diameter), lacing cord to make the cable looms

## Tools

- Combination pliers, side cutters, wire strippers, cutter knife
- Set of screwdrivers, hammer
- Voltage tester screwdriver, multimeter or clamp meter

## Sequence of work

1. Fix the equipment, rails and trunking according to the layout drawing.
2. Wire the circuit, making cable looms where needed.
3. Check that everything is firmly fixed.
4. Test the circuit dead with the measuring instruments (multimeter, voltage tester), then the power supply.
5. Test the circuit live if the dead tests are satisfactory.
6. Write a practical report.

**Estimated time**: 2 hours per learner after a demonstration.

## Assessment points

- The circuit works
- Equipment firmly fixed
- Learner's dexterity
- Layout drawing respected
- Neat work (straight runs and cable looms)
