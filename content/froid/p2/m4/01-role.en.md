## Purpose of a double switch

A double switch has **two one-way switching rockers**. From a single place, it makes and breaks **two separate circuits**, together or separately.

## Schematic diagram

The line supplies both switches; each switch output supplies its own group of lamps. The neutral goes straight to both groups of lamps. The exact wiring depends on the switch terminals: follow the manufacturer's instructions.

## Operation

- The first rocker switches lamp group 1 on or off.
- The second rocker switches lamp group 2 on or off.
- Both groups can be on together or separately.
